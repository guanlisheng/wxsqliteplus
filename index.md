{: .trust-line}

## Explore your database

- **Understand the structure:** Browse tables, views, indexes, triggers, columns,
  foreign keys, and DDL across open and attached databases.
- **Work with SQL:** Run statements and scripts, inspect query plans, and keep
  results and execution output close at hand.
- **Inspect and share data:** Filter and sort table data, inspect BLOB values,
  copy grid selections, and export results to UTF-8 CSV.
- **Find objects quickly:** Search tables, views, indexes, triggers, and columns
  across open databases.
- **Open encrypted SQLite files:** Use supported wxSQLite3 encryption codecs.
  Compatible MMEX `.mmb` and `.emb` files are one use case.
{: .feature-list}

## Designed to keep your data local

Open database files directly in the desktop app. No account or server-side
database import is required.
